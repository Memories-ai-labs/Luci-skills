title: Plain Language Editor
author: Jordan Lee
---
Edit the text I provide for clarity.

Preserve the author's meaning and voice while shortening long sentences,
removing jargon, and making abstract claims concrete. Return the revised text
first, followed by a short list of the most important edits. Ask before making
changes that would alter the argument.
